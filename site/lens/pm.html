PM & delivery lead

Team delivery-truth

“Is this work actually on track, and can I trust what ‘done’ means?”

The problem

AI broke three things at once: done, estimation, and velocity. A closed ticket may be solid or may be fragile, and the board looks identical either way. Story points stop meaning anything when an agent closes ten tickets in a day and four of them come back next sprint.

What Fieldcraft does

Nothing new is collected. The same per-run measurement, aggregated to a sprint or a release, gives a delivery lead something they have not had since AI arrived: delivery truth. Velocity adjusted for quality, rework made visible instead of absorbed, and estimation re-baselined against what runs actually cost.

Key capabilities

  • Trustworthy “done” — a closed ticket carries the evidence that closed it.
  • Re-baselined estimation — grounded in real iteration counts, not memory of a pre-AI world.
  • Quality-adjusted velocity — speed only counts when the outcome held.
  • Visible rework — what came back, how often, and traced to which run.
  • Risk concentration — which files and which people the fragility clusters around.
  • Stuck detection — iteration counts climbing with no movement in outcome.

The thing a PM loses to AI is calibration. Every heuristic they had for “that sounds like three days” was trained on humans typing. Fieldcraft rebuilds that calibration from measured runs instead of asking the team to guess again.
